# Offer structure

1. One paragraph on what the client wants to achieve.
2. Scope: what is included, in days.
3. What is not included.
4. Price: daily rate, days, total, valid for 30 days.
5. Next step and who to call.

One page. No discount unless the sales lead agreed to it.
