title: Workspaces
description: Your personal workspace, shared workspaces, and who sees what.
---

A workspace is where your conversations and Apps live. The German interface shows the labels in quotes.

- Your **personal workspace**, shown as "Anna's workspace" ("Annas Arbeitsbereich"), is yours alone.
- A **shared workspace** has members. Its conversations, agents and Apps are there for the team.

## Switch, create or join

Open the workspace menu in the main rail (**Switch workspace**, "Arbeitsbereich wechseln").

- Pick a workspace to switch to it. The rail then shows its agents, Apps and conversations.
- **Create** ("Erstellen") makes a new shared workspace. You are its owner.
- **Browse** ("Durchsuchen") lists the shared workspaces you may find. **Request access** ("Zugriff anfragen") asks their owners to let you in.

## Who sees what

In a shared workspace the owners choose, under **Settings > Workspace > General**, whether **New conversations are** ("Neue Unterhaltungen sind") **Private to their author** or **Visible to the workspace** ("Für den Arbeitsbereich sichtbar"). A change applies to new conversations only.

Published Apps of a workspace are open to all its members. Which agents a workspace offers is set in Studio.

## Manage members

Owners manage a shared workspace under **Settings > Workspace** ("Einstellungen › Arbeitsbereich"):

- **Members** ("Mitglieder"): **Add member** ("Mitglied hinzufügen"), give someone the role **Owner** ("Besitzer"), **Admin** ("Administrator") or **Member** ("Mitglied"), or remove them. Open access requests wait here too.
- **General** ("Allgemein"): the name, whether the workspace is **Discoverable** ("Auffindbar") or **Private** ("Privat"), and who sees new conversations.
