title: Your first conversation
description: Pick an agent, start a chat, add files, and find your way around Catalyst.
---

In Catalyst you work with **agents**: assistants set up for a task, such as answering client questions or drafting offers. You talk to one in a chat. The German interface shows the labels in quotes.

## Start a chat

1. Click **Agents** ("Agenten") in the main rail. It lists the agents of your current workspace.
2. Pick one to start a chat with it. **New chat** ("Neuer Chat") in the main rail starts a new chat at any time.
3. Type what you need and press Enter.

To add files, click **Add attachment** ("Anhang hinzufügen") or drop them onto the chat.

If the agent offers more than one model, the chat shows a picker with **Model** ("Modell") and **Reasoning** ("Denkaufwand"). More reasoning takes longer and uses more of your quota.

## Find it again

**Recent** ("Zuletzt") in the main rail lists your conversations, and **Search** ("Suchen") finds them. A conversation may be deleted after a while, as your organisation set it; the chat then says "Will be deleted automatically on …" ("Wird am … automatisch gelöscht").

## What appears beside the chat

- When the agent builds something for you, such as a small tool with screens, it shows it beside the chat. You can keep it as an App: see [Apps for people](/automate/apps/).
- When you open a chat from another page, for example with **Edit with the agent** on an App, the chat stays open on the left of that page as you move on. **Close chat** ("Chat schließen") hides it, **Chat** brings it back, and **Open as full chat** ("Als ganzen Chat öffnen") gives it the whole window.

## The rest of the main rail

- **Apps**: the Apps of your workspace.
- **Workflows**: work the platform does by itself. See [Workflows](/automate/workflows/).
- **Inbox** ("Eingang"): requests waiting for an approval.
- **Studio**: where agents, skills and connections to other systems are made. It shows only for people who build them. On an agent's card, **Edit in Studio** ("Im Studio bearbeiten") opens it there.
- **Settings** ("Einstellungen"): your profile, language, and your API keys.
