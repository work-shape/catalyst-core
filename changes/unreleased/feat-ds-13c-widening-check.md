- **Widening check (DS-13c):** At every change that widens who reads a store's rows or adds a source to it, the instance checks that everyone who reads the rows may use each of its sources (`connection.use`). The changes are the publish of a Workflow, an App (with the Workflows published with it), a Knowledge Base or an agent, an App's audience, `assets.set_availability` of a Knowledge Base, `assets.move`, `agents.give_tools`, and an agent made with its tools through `assets.put` or `assets.sync`. A Knowledge Base that an agent's consent (`makeDependenciesAvailable`) offers in more workspaces is checked like its own availability change. A new agent, or one that awaits its first publish, counts as nothing before, so each source it adds is new. A Knowledge Base with synced sources counts as a store of its own, read by whoever may use it. The publish question says in one line that the check holds. Where it does not, it says per source how many readers may not use it, never who.
- A change that newly hands a source's data to such readers waits in the Inbox, and no policy turns that off. The approver must be a person who also holds `connection.write` on each source, and their approval is the confirmation. The Inbox item shows the approver the same lines the caller saw: how many people, the store and the source, with titles only where the approver may see them.
- A person who holds `connection.write` can confirm directly with `confirmHandover` (from the question's `publishHandover.confirmation`) on `assets.publish`, `assets.set_availability`, `assets.move` or `agents.give_tools`. API keys, service principals and agents are refused (`data_handover_person_only`), because a key may be driven by an agent; so is a stale confirmation (`data_handover_unconfirmed`). The CLI has no confirmation flag: its publish help says that a hand-over waits in the Inbox for a person.
- When the check cannot read every published asset, it counts that as a gap and never lets the change pass silently.
- Each confirmed hand-over is audited as `data_store.handover_confirmed`, with a count of people, in the transaction that makes the change; a move filed as a request records it when the move is made.
- The check runs on the server; the interface only shows its answer, in German and English. An agent's publish card says that a person confirms the hand-over in the Publish dialog.
