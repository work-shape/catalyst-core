section: Changed
---

- **Apps:** An App's page head has one chat button. "Edit in chat" is gone: for a person who may edit the App, "Chat" opens the agent that builds beside the draft, in a new chat of their own that holds the draft with all its files, or brings back the dock's chat about this App. For a person who may only use the App, "Chat" opens their last chat. The running dot stays on "Chat".
