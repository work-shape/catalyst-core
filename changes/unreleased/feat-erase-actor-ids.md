- **Accounts:** Deleting an account now empties every stored copy of the person's name, e-mail address and identity-provider id outside the audit log, in the same transaction that deletes the account: on approval requests and their decisions and reversions, on held calls (including how the caller was signed in and the audience person an App publish names), on approval decision notes in Conversations (the metadata and the note the model reads), on operation runs and their decisions, on Workflow Runs, on asset revisions, on handled feedback, on guardrail and live-once switches, and on the Conversations the person created. The account id beside each copy stays. The person's own connection sign-ins are deleted. A repeated or interrupted deletion ends in the same state. The audit log keeps the name until `retention.auditDays`.
- **Accounts:** Wherever a person is named by their account, the name is read as the account has it now. An account that is gone, or whose deletion was requested, is shown as "Deleted account" (German "Gelöschtes Konto"), never as a blank or the account's id: in the Inbox, in approval decisions in chat, on Workflow Runs, in an asset's history, in Review on a Data Store and on the operations page. Text a model reads says "a deleted account".
- **Repository:** A schema test fails when a new column copies a person's label or actor and is neither erased on deletion nor listed as kept, with a reason.
