- **Apps:** History no longer counts a save that became a version as a draft save too. An App saved once and published once shows "Version 1" alone; under each version are only the saves made after it, and a publish of an earlier version again is its own version, with "Publish this version again" on an earlier one. The list and the draft head name the same states the same way: "Draft · not published" and "Draft: changed" in place of "Draft only" and "Unpublished changes", and the draft line says "Version 4 published by …" instead of "Published by …".
