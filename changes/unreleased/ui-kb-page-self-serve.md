section: Changed
---

- **Knowledge:** the page of a Knowledge Base of documents is its documents. Its description is
  the lead line under the name. One line says how many documents are searchable ("63 of 65
  documents are searchable") and, only where something needs the reader, how many could not be
  read or are still being read; beside it stands the one action, **Add documents**, which
  uploads files and, where the instance offers synced sources, connects a folder. In the list,
  what failed stands first with its reason and Retry, then what is still being read; a
  searchable document says nothing of its state. While the list is read a page at a time, only
  the documents read so far are ordered so, and the line says what the instance knows of all of
  them. Each synced folder is one row at the top of the list with one sentence in place of its
  schedule ("Keeps itself up to date: new and changed files every 15 minutes, deleted files are
  removed every night."); its folders, schedule and sync state wait behind **Adjust**, which
  opens by itself where a sync needs a person. The pass that removes deleted files is no longer
  called a full sync: it lists the source by name and date and reads no unchanged file again.
  Everything else (name and description, availability, agents, access, status with re-index,
  test search, keyword languages, data flows) is folded under **Settings** at the end of the
  page; a refused save opens it at the field. The page has no outline. A Knowledge Base of
  tables keeps its layout with the same lead line and Settings, and a long table name under
  Status wraps instead of running into its badge.
- **UI library:** `Disclosure` has a `row` variant, a line as wide as its column with the arrow
  at its end.
