- **Workflows:** One Publish of a Workflow makes it live with the Data Stores its steps call (LIVE-2). The publish names each store at the reviewed revision (`dataStores`, as on an App's Publish); each store is published and its pending migrations applied first, then the Workflow's own account and grants are created or updated and the revision goes live. Before any store moves, the Workflow's own checks that read no store run first: its limits, mail steps, outside-start switch, packages, cycles, person contexts and reach. These come from the new asset kind hook `checkBeforePublish`. When a migration fails, nothing goes live and the refusal says the Workflow was not published (`publishing: "workflow"`). A Workflow's Publish waits only for the stores it names. A held publish holds its stores with it, in one Inbox item. The read of a Workflow lists the stores the reader may publish with their pending migrations (`publishDataStores`), and the Workflow page's publish question names them above the account line. A store's own Publish is unchanged.
