A Connect card in chat: when an agent proposes a catalogue entry or an OAuth Connection, or a call of a personal Connection is refused because the person has not signed in or must sign in again, the conversation's owner sees "<Service> verbinden" with one button. Connect signs in through a popup (or a new tab where popups are blocked) and then sends "<Service> ist verbunden" as the person, so the agent carries on. A Connection that is not set up offers Einrichten to people who may write Connections and asks everyone else to contact their administration. Refused tool results carry a host-only `connect` field with the Connection's name, title, service and catalogue link, never an account or a token.
